# InkMark Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期: September 16, 2026 / 2026 年 9 月 16 日

## English

### Overview

InkMark respects your privacy. In the current version, photo processing, EXIF reading, watermark rendering, and recipe storage occur locally on your device. Photos and personal information are not uploaded to developer servers.

### Data we process

When you select a photo, the app may read its contents and embedded EXIF data, such as device brand, device model, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, and photo location. You may also enter a signature and commonly used devices. This information is used only for watermark features you choose.

### Local storage

Language, signature, devices, and watermark recipes are stored locally by the app. Temporary files may be created in the app cache while processing a photo. Exported photos are saved to the system photo library only after your action. Uninstalling normally clears local app data but does not remove previously exported photos.

### Permissions

Photo selection and library permissions are used to read photos you choose and save exports. Media location permission is used, when supported and granted, to access location metadata in the original photo. Denying an optional permission does not affect features that do not depend on it.

### Collection and sharing

The current version has no user accounts or advertising. It does not transmit, sell, or share your photos, EXIF data, signature, device list, or recipes with the developer or third-party servers. Data accessed and processed only on your device is not collected by the developer.

### Third-party services

The app uses operating-system photo selection and library-saving services. If in-app purchases, crash analytics, or other online services are introduced, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated to describe their data handling.

### Children's privacy

InkMark is not primarily directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Guardians may contact us at qchunk@163.com.

### Security and changes

We use reasonable measures to protect local app data. This policy may change when features or data practices change. Material updates will be communicated in the app or release notes.

### Contact

For privacy or data-handling questions, contact us at qchunk@163.com.

## 简体中文

### 概述

InkMark 重视你的隐私。当前版本的照片处理、EXIF 读取、水印生成和配方保存均在你的设备本地完成，不会将照片或个人信息上传至开发者服务器。

### 我们处理的数据

你主动选择照片后，应用可能读取照片内容以及其中包含的 EXIF 信息，例如设备品牌、设备型号、焦距、光圈、快门、ISO 和照片拍摄位置。你也可以在设置中填写署名和常用设备。这些信息仅用于你选择的水印功能。

### 本地存储

语言、署名、常用设备和水印配方保存在应用的本地存储中。处理照片时可能在应用缓存目录创建临时文件；导出的照片仅在你主动操作后保存到系统相册。卸载应用通常会清除应用本地数据，但已导出到相册的照片不会被自动删除。

### 权限说明

相册或照片选择权限用于读取你主动选择的照片以及保存导出结果。媒体位置权限用于在系统允许且你授权时读取照片原始文件中的位置元数据。拒绝非必要权限不会影响未依赖该权限的功能。

### 数据收集与共享

当前版本不运营用户账号、不包含广告，也不会向开发者或第三方服务器传输、出售或共享你的照片、EXIF、署名、设备列表或配方。仅在设备本地访问和处理的数据不构成向开发者收集。

### 第三方服务

应用使用操作系统提供的照片选择和相册保存能力。若未来接入应用内购买、崩溃分析或其他联网服务，本政策和 Google Play 数据安全声明将同步更新，并说明相关服务处理的数据。

### 儿童隐私

InkMark 不以儿童为主要目标用户，也不会主动收集儿童个人信息。监护人如发现相关问题，可通过 qchunk@163.com 联系我们。

### 安全与变更

我们采用合理措施保护应用本地数据。功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策可能更新；重要变化会通过应用内说明或商店版本信息告知。

### 联系我们

如对隐私政策或数据处理有疑问，请发送邮件至 qchunk@163.com。
